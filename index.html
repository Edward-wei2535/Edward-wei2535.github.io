About Me

I work on lightweight object detection for agricultural vision — recognising and grading mushrooms, apples, blueberries, cherry tomatoes and ginseng in real field conditions, where variable light, occlusion and densely packed small objects break off-the-shelf detectors.

I received my M.S. from Jilin Agricultural University in 2026 and have published eight SCI papers, including first-author work in Ecological Informatics. I am currently applying for Ph.D. programmes.
